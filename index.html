Trade Log / Catatan Trade

Open / Buka

Add one trade at a time. Use the same name and secret code as your card. / Tambah satu trade setiap kali. Pakai nama dan kode rahasia yang sama dengan kartumu.

Write prices as on the chart, not the word "entry". Each entry has its own lot, SL, TP1 to TP3 and its own result, because averaging entries often close differently. Press + for each averaging. / Tulis harga seperti di chart, bukan tulisan "entry". Tiap entry punya lot, SL, TP1 sampai TP3, dan hasilnya sendiri, karena entry averaging sering tertutup berbeda. Tekan + untuk tiap averaging.
Closed by = how this entry actually ended: TP (hit a target), SL (stopped out at the original SL), SL+ (stopped out after you moved SL to entry or into profit), Manual close (you closed it yourself). / Cara entry ini benar-benar berakhir: TP (kena target), SL (kena SL awal), SL+ (kena SL setelah digeser ke entry atau profit), Manual close (kamu tutup sendiri).
Best price reached = the best price the market touched in your favour while this entry was open (highest for Buy, lowest for Sell), even if it came back later. If it never moved in your favour, write the entry price. The form then works out Max R, the highest TP reached and Hit 1:1 by itself. / Harga terbaik yang disentuh market searah posisimu selama entry ini terbuka (tertinggi untuk Buy, terendah untuk Sell), walau harga kemudian berbalik. Jika tidak pernah searah, tulis harga entry. Form menghitung sendiri Max R, TP tertinggi, dan Hit 1:1.
